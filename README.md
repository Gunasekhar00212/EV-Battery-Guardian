# ⚡ EV Battery Guardian

### 🧠 AI-Based Predictive Battery Health & Early Alert System for Electric Vehicles

---

## 🚀 Overview
**EV Battery Guardian** is an AI-powered system designed to make electric vehicles smarter and safer.  
It predicts battery health and gives early charging alerts **before** the battery level becomes critical, ensuring the driver never misses a nearby charging station.

Unlike traditional systems that alert only after the battery drops below a set limit, this model uses **machine learning** to forecast the future battery level based on real-time parameters like voltage, current, temperature, and driving behavior.

---

## 🌍 The Problem
Imagine driving your EV peacefully, music on, smooth ride — then suddenly, the low-battery warning appears.  
You realize the last charging station you passed was 5 km behind, and the next one is 30 km ahead.  
Now you’re stuck deciding whether to turn back or risk running out of power.

This happens because current EVs **alert too late** — after the battery crosses the danger threshold.

---

## 💡 The Solution
**EV Battery Guardian** acts like a smart companion sitting beside you.  
It constantly watches your battery, predicts how long it will last, checks nearby stations, and warns you *early* if you might miss a safe charging spot.  
This keeps you confident, safe, and never stranded.

---
